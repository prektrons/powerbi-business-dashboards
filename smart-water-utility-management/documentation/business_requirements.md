# Business Requirements

## Project Title

Smart Water Utility Intelligence Dashboard — South Korea

## 1. Business Problem

Water-quality monitoring produces large volumes of measurements
across different monitoring stations and time periods.

Environmental and water-management teams need an efficient way
to monitor water-quality conditions, identify locations with
potential deterioration, understand seasonal patterns, and
prioritize areas that may require further investigation.

The objective of this project is to transform Korean water-quality
monitoring data into an interactive business intelligence
dashboard using Microsoft Power BI.

## 2. Project Objective

The dashboard will provide a centralized analytical view of
water-quality conditions across monitored water resources in
Jeonbuk, South Korea.

The solution will help users:

- Monitor key water-quality indicators
- Compare monitoring stations
- Identify high-risk locations
- Analyze trends over time
- Detect seasonal patterns
- Investigate potential deterioration
- Support data-driven environmental decisions

## 3. Key Business Questions

### Overall Water Quality

1. What is the overall condition of monitored water resources?
2. How many monitoring stations are included?
3. How many water-quality measurements are available?

### Water Quality Parameters

4. What are the average levels of BOD, COD and DO?
5. Which stations have unusually high BOD or COD?
6. Which stations have unusually low dissolved oxygen?
7. Which locations have elevated nitrogen or phosphorus levels?

### Time Analysis

8. How does water quality change over time?
9. Are there seasonal patterns?
10. Which parameters show increasing or decreasing trends?

### Geographic Analysis

11. Which monitoring stations have the highest risk?
12. Are certain areas consistently showing poorer water quality?

### Decision Support

13. Which stations should environmental managers investigate first?
14. Which water-quality parameters contribute most to overall risk?

## 4. Target Users

- Environmental managers
- Water-quality analysts
- Government environmental organizations
- Water resource managers
- Data analysts
- Business intelligence professionals

## 5. Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard will initially consider:

- Total monitoring stations
- Total measurements
- Average water temperature
- Average pH
- Average dissolved oxygen
- Average BOD
- Average COD
- Average SS
- Average TN
- Average TP
- High-risk stations

## 6. Future Analytical Features

The project may later include:

- Water-quality risk scoring
- Anomaly detection
- Trend detection
- Early-warning indicators
- Station ranking
- Geographic risk analysis
